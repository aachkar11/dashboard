Runner Profile
Aerobic Base 101
🟢
Keep ~80% easy. Easy means you can hold a full conversation. If you can't, slow down or take walk breaks — it still builds the base.
📈
The 10% rule. Raise weekly distance by no more than ~10% a week, and take a lighter week roughly every fourth week.
🗓
Consistency beats intensity. Three or four steady weeks in a row does more for your base than one heroic week.
🦵
Rest is training. As a beginner, 2–3 full rest or cross-training days a week is normal and protects your shins and knees.
📝
Write notes every day. How the run felt, sleep, soreness, weather. That's the context that makes an analysis actually useful.
Danger Zone
Erase every logged run, plan entry and daily note. This can't be undone.
